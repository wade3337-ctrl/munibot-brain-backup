# The Usual Suspects — SoCal Municipal Tree Bidders

The same ~7 firms bid almost every municipal tree contract in LA/Orange/Riverside/San Bernardino counties. Knowing their pricing patterns lets you predict the competitive floor before opening a single bid result.

## The field (from Pomona Oct 2025 — 7 bidders)

| Bidder | Pomona 2025 Annual | Pattern | Notes |
|--------|-------------------|---------|-------|
| **West Coast Arborists (WCA)** | $904K (won) | **The incumbent to beat.** Holds 200+ SoCal municipalities. Species/district-sensitive grid pricing. Sometimes bids loss-leaders on small-tree Full Prune ($93 on 0-18 when median is $170). Overprices mid-size removals (13-18: $843 vs median $720). Bid $0 on Prune Backup once (error/oversight). | Held Pomona since 2000, Bell Gardens 2020-2025, Long Beach Public Works 2021-2026. Cooperative-purchase heavyweight. |
| **Golden West Arbor Services** | $565K (lowest) | **The low-ball threat.** Often 30-40% under the field. Suspiciously low — may be non-responsive or under-resourced. Don't chase their numbers; assume they're not a real floor. | Bids $60-68 flat on grid regardless of species. |
| **Innovative Tree Company** | $643K | **The odd-unit bidder.** Prices removals per-inch-DSH instead of per-tree. Hard to compare directly — convert using band midpoints. | Palm trunk clean priced per-foot (correct UOM). |
| **North Star Land Care** | ~$1.1M | Mid-to-high. Mid-pack on most lines. | Often bids per-hour on items others bid flat. |

## WCA pricing signatures (the incumbent to beat)

- **Grid:** species/district-sensitive, NOT flat. Ranges $83 (Street) to $233 (Chinese Elm) on the same contract. They know which trees are expensive to prune and price accordingly.
- **Small-tree Full Prune (0-18):** often bids anomalously low ($93 in Pomona 2025, median was $170). Likely a loss leader or grid-efficiency play. **Don't chase it** — stay near the field median.
- **Mid-size Tree & Stump Removal (13-18):** consistently OVERprices ($843 vs field median $720). We can beat them here AND keep margin.
- **Specialty Equipment Rental:** bids high (~$993/day vs our $495). Probably a coop-purchase artifact.
- **Crew Backup lines:** has bid $0 on some (Prune Backup in Pomona 2025). Treat as an error, not a real price — bid your real crew cost.
- **Hourly/TPH:** ~$120-143/man-hr range. Their TPH has risen alongside ours (was $94 in 2021).

## How to use this

1. When a new SoCal muni bid comes in, expect **WCA + 4-6 of the others** to bid. The floor is WCA, not Golden West.
2. Don't anchor on Golden West's low numbers — they're often non-credible.
3. If we're above Mariposa on any line, come down.
4. Check WCA's prior bid in the warehouse `Bid Results.xlsx` — their pricing signatures are consistent across cities.
5. On **qualification-weighted** RFPs (Bell Gardens, 2021 Pomona), WCA's incumbency + their narrative wins even when we're cheaper. Compete on qualification, not just price.
