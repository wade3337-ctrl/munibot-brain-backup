## WCA's pricing patterns (the benchmark to beat)

### Extra Work (per ea) — WCA's anomalies
- **Full Prune 0-18: $93** — below everyone (median ~$170). Likely a loss leader to win service-request volume. **Don't chase.**
- **Crown Raise: $93** — same as their Full Prune 0-18 (they treat it as a small-tree prune)
- **Tree & Stump Removal 13-18: $843** — HIGHEST of all 7 bidders (median ~$720). WCA overpriced mid-band removals. **Opportunity to beat them AND keep margin.**
- **Tree Only Removal 13-18 through 36+: $843/$1293/$1793/$2193/$2993** — identical to their Tree & Stump prices. They don't discount for stump-left-in. **Opportunity.**
- **Prune Backup – Crew: $0** — error/oversight, not a real price. Ignore as a comp.
- **Specialty Equipment Rental: $993/day** — 2× our $495. They don't want this work.
- **Arborist Reports / Risk Assessment: $193/hr** — premium. We hold at $149.
- **All chemical apps (Canopy Spray, Trunk Banding, Soil, Injection): $10/unit** — flat across all. Commodity pricing.

### Planting (per ea) — WCA
- 15 gal: $253 | 24" box: $493 | 36" box: $1,393 | 48" box: $2,493 | 60" box: $4,993
- Their 36" and 48" are HIGH vs the field (median ~$1,100 / ~$2,000). We can beat them.

## What the 7-bidder field tells us about the market

- **Hourly/crew rates cluster at $97–$130/man-hr.** Innovative $82, Mario $97, North Star $100, WCA $120, Mariposa $120, GSTS $129, Golden West $320 (outlier). Our $129 is mid-pack, not high.
- **DIR prevailing wage confirmed** — everyone pays the same Davis-Bacon rates. TPH differences reflect overhead structure and crew efficiency, not labor cost.
- **Removal pricing splits into two camps:** per-tree (WCA, GSTS, Mariposa, North Star) vs per-inch (Innovative, Golden West partial). Per-tree is the norm for fixed-scope municipal work.
- **Chemical/spray lines converge at $7–$10/unit** across all bidders. Commodity — don't waste margin cutting here.

## Re-bid context (IFB# 2026-17)

- Re-solicted after the Oct 2025 round. Form restructured: now Level A / Level B, separate grid per category, palm trunk clean UOM changed to per-foot.
- **Award = lowest responsive bid** (RFP §8). No qualification scoring this round (unlike 2021).
- WCA may or may not re-bid — 25-year incumbent, but the re-solicit suggests friction. If they don't bid, the floor rises and we can come up.
